# Musicmarket

MusicMarket streamlines the concert ticket experience by simplifying discovery, making pricing transparent, and removing confusion around resale and transfers. The design is shaped by real concertgoer needs and iterative user testing.

## Core Features
- Event discovery with genre, date, and price filtering  
- Clear primary vs resale ticket distinction  
- Transparent pricing and market insights  
- StockX-style resale marketplace with bids and asks  
- Safe, trackable ticket transfers  
- Smooth navigation across discovery, purchase, and management flows  

## User Needs
- Easier ways to find concerts that match interests and budget  
- Direct clarity on pricing, resale dynamics, and ticket types  
- A trustworthy transfer flow that preserves confidence and ownership  
- A navigation structure that connects discovery, buying, and reselling naturally  

## Design Process
We explored concertgoer behavior through needfinding, sketched multiple conceptual alternatives, iterated designs based on feedback, and validated flows through structured user testing. The final product reflects repeated refinement toward clearer information, simpler interactions, and stronger trust signals.

## Key Design Decisions
- A two-track layout that mirrors how users think: primary buying on one side, market data on the other.  
- StockX-inspired resale patterns to help users understand bids, asks, and price trends.  
- A guided transfer flow with reassurance and tracking to reduce fear of losing tickets.

## User Feedback and Iteration
Feedback pushed the design toward clearer language, more transparent pricing indicators, and stronger onboarding for distinguishing primary tickets from resale listings. We also improved transfer messaging after users expressed concern about ownership loss.

## User Testing Insights
Testing showed that avid concertgoers wanted deeper event info, while budget-focused users needed simpler explanations of marketplace mechanics. These insights led to clearer section labels, trust messaging, and price-first sorting.
